## Shared direction

Make Highlights and support easy for families and communities. Beyond the Game adds workouts, service, learning and everyday stories that help fans learn about athletes; participation and service remain voluntary. Fan value includes browsing, permitted uploads and friendships without donating or becoming a payee. Growth plans cover overlapping user segments, with no claimed retention/conversion effect until measured.

- **Release:** Wave 0 internal/test mode; Utah is the first planned US rollout. Planning for the rest of the country proceeds in parallel; each subsequent rollout needs its own legal/school/payment review. There is no nationwide release approval.
- **Local implementation:** dedicated Beyond tab, four categories and reviewed contributions; CHAL-01a self-reported workouts/preset responses/invites/private progress; UX-06 first presentation pass and newer Calm Island colour refresh. Written migrations/default-off server controls are not hosted availability. See [BEYOND-01](BEYOND-01.md), [CHAL-01](CHAL-01.md), [UX-06](UX-06.md) and STATE's latest colour receipt.
- **Newer design source:** the original UX-06 Figma draft is empty. A separate Calm Island Figma colour file is recorded in STATE; it does not populate the original draft. New source supersedes older appearance/manifests; old tests/screenshots remain dated evidence. Published runtime colours can still override the local preset. DESIGN/ACCESSIBILITY/QA own current rendered/native review; publishing controls requires a separate authorized action.
- **Still proposed:** fan Game/Week/Month/Year awards and generic badges, free fan-voted winners, school competitions, voluntary service activities and timed trivia (opt-in invitation, 30 seconds with a longer accessible option). Existing counters, Aura and workouts do not establish these capabilities. [Family playbook](../product/FAMILY_FUN_PLAYBOOK.md) and [per-user plans](../product/USER_GROWTH_PLANS.md) separate proposals from local implementation.
- **Admin:** Expo `/admin` owns production writes; Next is read-only oversight. Earlier custom CSV/XLSX, date ranges and Product workspace work in the separate fan-zone checkout remains unintegrated until a scoped handoff verifies current mounts and ownership. Finance exports already in MAIN are a separate capability.
- **Money and youth:** test Stripe, blocked payouts, no parallel client/webhook credit path; applicable guardian/athlete/school checks remain action-specific. SSNs stay with Stripe. Public youth content, payment and release gates need the relevant legal/privacy/safety/security/finance evidence.
- **Operations:** local Support/Health tooling and declared sensors exist, with known gaps. They do not prove complete traffic accounting, continuous crash/delivery monitoring, hosted recovery or settlement reconciliation. No SOC 2/HIPAA certification, uptime SLA or legal launch clearance is established by this review.
