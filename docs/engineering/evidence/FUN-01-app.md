## Actual account journey

The root `/fan-zone` route has Join the fun, Meet an athlete and Serve together paths,
explicit Back/account Home and a validated optional campaign query. An account sees only
its server-authorized campaign, own entry and selected campaign guardian review queue.
That private queue is independent of eligibility to participate and ordinary invitation
dismissal. Home and four role HQs keep its review shortcut; profile has a generic Fan Zone
entry. The selected campaign query preserves context. It is not an all-campaign guardian inbox. A missing or refused snapshot does not become public candidate access.

A preserved in-memory draft reserves a private revision, compresses the clip, inserts bytes
without overwrite and calls the authorized verification API. Verification must match its
revision, digest, bounded bytes and duration before the exact permission can be reviewed.
No public reel upload or thumbnail fallback is used. Uncertain uploads verify the existing
revision before retrying; a replacement reserves a new revision.

Permission review requires the matching uncached authorized player readiness and first-frame
signals, plus separate caption and exact scope confirmation. A teen's assent and a linked
guardian's exact grant are separate; revocation, owner withdrawal and stored-media removal
are separate actions. Playback readiness does not prove a whole clip was watched, every
subject's rights or legal guardianship. Stale refusal immediately invalidates the old snapshot and advances a private review generation
before the authoritative read. Even an equal returned scope requires fresh video and caption
review; an unavailable read exposes no old grant-capable controls.

Valid free account ballots select the result. An uncertain ballot retains its original
request, candidate and expected version; an authoritative fresh read establishes the current
ballot. Ties and zero valid votes have explicit outcomes. Staff review entry eligibility,
rights and safety and can invalidate proven bad ballots with reasons and recount.

Curated free athlete stories open the same existing profile and Donate path. Optional service
uses private presets and deletable reflections; it creates no cash, Aura, vote advantage or
public badge. Help & Safety provides actual concern/status controls, bounded private details,
selectable receipt secrets and an authorized configured contact when available. Policy text
does not receive a report. A staffed public guest fallback remains release work.

## Release work

Both features remain paused/draft, testing-only and empty by default, with at most 100 approved
canonical testers. SQL is written but not applied. Countries/ages, facts, contest/consent and
retention terms, school/subject permissions, legal guardian assurance, staffing, API origin and
server deployment, real provider access/removal/range costs, browser/native/accessibility and
approved database recovery remain open. Already delivered bytes cannot be recalled. In-memory
drafts and secrets can be lost on unmount; users must save selectable receipt details.
