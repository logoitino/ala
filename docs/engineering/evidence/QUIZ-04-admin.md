# QUIZ-04 admin-ready evidence (2026-10-15)

Feature: Life Playbook and personal progress. Covers opt-in life-skills trivia, separate weekly allowances, and levels
that only go up. Design: [LIFE_PLAYBOOK_DESIGN.md](../design/LIFE_PLAYBOOK_DESIGN.md) (review §7 adopted). Ledger:
[QUIZ-04](../../agents/QUIZ-04.md).

## What was built for the admin stage

- **Source.** Two migrations, both written and **not applied anywhere**, copied onto `schema.sql`:
  - `20261014180000_life_playbook.sql`;
  - `20261014190000_life_playbook_bank.sql` (56 drafts, generated by `scripts/life-bank-sql.mjs` from
    `supabase/content/life_playbook_bank.json`).
- **Rounds.**
  - Separate personal / group / life weekly guards, with uniqueness per account, week and kind. Still one open round at
    a time.
  - Per-kind play budgets plus a separate save budget.
  - `quiz_life_start` is the only way into a Life round.
  - Group "played", Family Challenger and group totals count group rounds only; a group round needs at least two
    members.
- **Content control.**
  - Named reviewers in `quiz_content_reviewers`: granted by one admin and confirmed by a second, never the grantee.
  - Approval needs an active reviewer for the item's domain who wrote no version of it.
  - Review fields are immutable.
  - One shared copy ruleset (`supabase/content/life_copy_rules.json`, embedded in SQL and checked against the JS) runs
    at approval and re-review. It normalizes the text first (NFKC, zero-width and space handling, macron-stripped
    matching, non-Latin letters refused). It enforces:
    - the chapter rules: scam ending, no "stranger", the NIL compliance-office line only, the Credit / Tax disclaimer,
      no health topics;
    - no unmarked figures for 13–17;
    - allow-listed `.gov` / nonprofit sources;
    - a tax-year window of last year, this year or next year.
- **Chapters.**
  - Seven chapters, all closed.
  - A chapter opens only with 20 current approved items. Credit Clock and Tax Timeout also need a confirmed CPA
    sign-off that covers those exact versions; tax sign-offs expire 31 January, credit sign-offs after 12 months.
- **Age and dates.**
  - The age band is checked at Start and at every Next. `not_given` / `unknown` accounts get 13+ chapters, and no
    message ever mentions unlocking.
  - Dated items are adult-only and withdrawn after 31 January (UTC).
- **Progress.** Levels keep the highest value reached, with admin-set thresholds; erasure clears them.
- **Privacy.**
  - Life stats are aggregate-only: at least 20 responders per question; band × chapter and level cells under 10 hidden.
  - Life activity is out of the trivia snapshot counts and its audit feed.
  - Audit rows keep chapter and position, never question ids.
  - Opt-in and Life badges are private.
- **Admin desk** (`/admin/sport-quiz`, `SportQuizLife` + `SportQuizLifeQuestion`):
  - the `trivia.life` switch;
  - chapters with the reason each is closed;
  - CPA sign-off record and confirm;
  - reviewer grant / confirm / revoke with reasons;
  - life question fields;
  - the re-review list;
  - level thresholds;
  - aggregate stats.

  Before the migration is applied, the panel says "not switched on".
- **Disable.** `trivia.life` is off by default. Switching it off ends open Life rounds without touching personal or
  group play. The feature inherits QUIZ-02's pause and empty cohort.

## Control tower and simple question flow (2026-10-15)

Owner request: trivia belongs in the main admin control tower, with easy adding and customizing of questions. Admin
only; no participant screen, no SQL and no new setting.

- **Placement.** The Operations menu item is now **Trivia** ("Questions, Life Playbook, groups and switches"); the route
  stays `/admin/sport-quiz`, and desk search finds it by trivia, quiz, questions, Life Playbook or the old name
  (`lib/admin.ts`). The admin Home shows a **Trivia status card** (`TriviaStatusCard`, supplied by the Home route through
  `components/Admin/AdminHomeCards.tsx`; `AdminReportHome.tsx` only marks the slot): trivia paused / on for testers /
  on, the groups / Life / images switches, drafts waiting, Life chapters open, topics and chapters running low, and a
  link to the desk. It uses only `quiz_admin_snapshot` and `quiz_admin_life_snapshot`; an unapplied read says "not
  switched on", never zero. The Control map adds This week, level and four Life Playbook rows, and the page Skip now reads
  "Skip my round this week" (`lib/adminCoverage.ts`).
- **Add a question** (`SportQuizAddQuestion`) is the bank's default view: topic (Sports with a sport picker, Science,
  Geography, Fun, Life Playbook with a chapter), type, question, answers, correct answer, short explanation, and for Life
  the education-only attestation. More options holds the family (default new), source note (default "Everyday fact")
  and reason (default "Added in admin"). Live checks show the server limits (Life 120 / 200, other topics 280 / 500)
  and the Life word rules in plain words before saving; a player preview shows True / False as two big answers.
- **One rule source.** The Life rule matching moved to `packages/shared-types/lifeCopyRules.ts`; `scripts/life-bank-sql.mjs`
  imports it (Node type stripping) and the app passes it `supabase/content/life_copy_rules.json`, still the only rule
  file. The bank SQL output is byte-identical and every probe passes through the shared code.
- **Drafts to review** (`SportQuizReview`) walks loaded drafts one at a time with the preview, explanation and source
  note; Approve, Edit, Archive and Next; filters by topic, sport or Life chapter; per-chapter counters ("Money Huddle: 3
  of 5 approved to open"); a session reason defaulting to "Content review". Each Approve / Archive is its own audited
  `quiz_admin_question` write. Edit saves a new version, then archives the draft it replaces (two audited writes; a
  failed archive is reported, not hidden). All questions keeps the full editor (images, rights review, void).
- **Settings** (`SportQuizSettings`) gathers trivia state and testers, round size and the groups switch (images shown
  locked off; a save sends it off), and the Life switch and level thresholds, each through its existing write.
