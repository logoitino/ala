# Gift, Donation and Tip — Terms draft

**Draft for counsel, 2026-10-22. Not lawyer-final, accepted terms or authority to launch.**
This replaces the older donation-everywhere/10%-share framing as a drafting instruction.
The older decisions remain in [LEGAL_BRIEFING](LEGAL_BRIEFING.md) and [STATE](../STATE.md).

## Proposed clauses

**Gift.** A Gift is a voluntary personal monetary gift to an individual athlete or their family.
It is separate from a Donation, Tip, Subscription or Purchase. Giving is optional and does not
buy digital content, preferential access, a performance, a result or an endorsement.

**Donation.** A Donation is a voluntary contribution to a booster club or school program,
through that program's web checkout. The recipient's own legal and tax status determines
whether a contribution qualifies for any deduction. Tapua'i does not promise a tax deduction;
the club supplies its status and receipt wording.

**Tip.** A Tip is optional monetary support for Tapua'i, added separately at checkout. It is
not part of a Gift or Donation and is not a required fee. A Tip does not buy an entitlement.
The optional control reads: “Tip Tapua'i (optional)”.

**Processing.** Optional “Cover the processing fee” is a separately disclosed contribution
toward Stripe processing. It is not a Tip. Counsel must review the actual amount, who bears
processing when coverage is declined, refunds and the distinction between gross gift allocation
and the recipient's net proceeds. No clause may conceal a processing deduction.

**Platform.** Tapua'i is a platform, not a charity, and is not a party to any Gift. Users choose
whether and to whom to give. Counsel must reconcile this clause with the actual merchant,
processor, custodial, transfer, refund and dispute responsibilities before finalizing it.

**Tax notice.** Gifts to individual athletes are personal gifts and are not tax-deductible.
This notice appears on the athlete page and gift receipt. It does not determine other tax,
reporting, eligibility or guardian obligations.

**Paid products.** Athlete premium is a Subscription. A paid digital extra is a Purchase.
They require separate terms and checkout; giving does not grant either. The current one-time
Exclusive payment must not be described as a recurring subscription.
